Everything a home juggles.

Our day

Agenda, tasks and widgets you arrange yourself. Week, month and agenda calendars, with each person's color.

Routines for kids

Weekdays, times, rewards, parent approval and whose turn it is, with history the kids can see.

Smart shopping

Several lists, categories and quantities, and suggestions from what you usually buy. Lists move from trip to trip.

Pixel characters

Kids earn XP and achievements, and unlock a cap and a moon cape that show up everywhere.

Ask your home

Claude answers from records you're allowed to see and prepares tasks, events or lists. Nothing is saved until you tap Apply.

Money, finally clear

Import bank and card statements, see where it went, catch recurring bills and price increases, and plan next month.

The whole household

Meals and pantry, maintenance on a schedule, and private documents with their own sharing.

Private by design

Every note, task and event has an owner and visibility. The server enforces it, and AI follows the same rules.

Talk to your home. It listens.

Your own Telegram bot sends reminders and understands text, photos and voice. Press play.

Yours, on your own cloud.

Bayit runs on your Cloudflare account (the free plan is enough for a family). Your Google sign-in, your Claude key, your Telegram bot. Nobody else's servers.

  1. 1
    Clone and install
    git clone https://github.com/frenkelsergei-blip/bayit.git
cd bayit/app && npm install
  2. 2
    Create D1, R2 and the Pages project
    npx wrangler d1 create bayit-home
npx wrangler r2 bucket create bayit-home-files
  3. 3
    Add your secrets and deploy
    npx wrangler d1 migrations apply bayit-home --remote
npm run deploy:cloud
